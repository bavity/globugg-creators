# Contributing to Globugg

Thanks for helping! Here's everything you need.

## Before you start

- **Only submit your own work.** No traced, AI-copied or borrowed art, brands, logos or real people's likeness without permission.
- **Keep it Globugg:** playful, welcoming, for ages 13+. Nothing hateful, sexual, violent or political. See the [Community guidelines](https://globugg.com/guidelines).
- **One idea per pull request** (one layer, trail, challenge, theme, frame, sticker pack or sound pack), so it's quick to review.

## Licence (please read)

By opening a pull request you confirm that you made the work and you license it under **[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)**. In plain words: Globugg (and anyone else) may use, change and share it, including in the app and in promotion, as long as you're credited. You keep your copyright and can use your work anywhere else.

## Step by step

1. **Fork** this repo (or use **Add file → Upload files** on GitHub, which forks for you).
2. Copy the example in the folder you're contributing to and follow that folder's `README.md`.
3. Put your name and GitHub username in `"author"`.
4. Optional: check it on your computer with `node scripts/validate.mjs` (Node 20+). The same check runs on your pull request.
5. Open a **pull request** and fill in the short template.

## What happens next

- The automatic check must pass (it tells you exactly what to fix).
- The Globugg team reviews within about a week. We may suggest changes or say no if it doesn't fit; we'll always explain why.
- Merged work goes live in the app, credited to you.

## Reporting bugs and ideas

Use the [issue templates](../../issues/new/choose). For anything private (security, someone's personal data), email support@globugg.com instead of opening an issue.
