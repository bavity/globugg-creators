# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their age, background, identity or experience.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique the work, never the person.
- No harassment, hate, sexual content, threats or sharing other people's private information.
- Keep discussions on topic and in English or the language of the contribution.

The Globugg team may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who don't follow this. To report something privately, email support@globugg.com.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
